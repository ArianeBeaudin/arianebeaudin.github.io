Hello, my name is Ariane Beaudin (they/she, iel/elle in French). 
I'm a writer-intellectual-witch-herborist-activist, also the founder of [Fleurs d'attache](https://www.instagram.com/fleursdattache/). In other words, I'm eternally a generalist. I try to anchor everything I do in the ethics of care.

My fields of interest include, but are not limited to decolonization, spirituality, radical imaginaries, the reenchantemnent of the world, urban agriculture, critical studies of psychology, community building and (non-monogamous) love.  

You can always reach out to me by email at ariane.beaudin@hotmail.com or with a DM on [Instagram](https://www.instagram.com/ariane.beaudin/). Au plaisir!
