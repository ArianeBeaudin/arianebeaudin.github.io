Some of thoses resulting pieces would include one named _to kill a politician with poisonous plants_ about (non-)violence and plants intelligencia and another one would be _the oracle of invisible demons_ which would be a speculative future design essay potentially accompanied by a short story. 

So themes that would be explored in this collection _future/ing plant magic_ would be : [time], [violence], [transnationalism], [plant agency], [plant communication], [spirituality/religion], [climate change], [narratives], [death], [anthropocene].
