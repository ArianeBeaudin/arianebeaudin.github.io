I love doing annual review to see how far I was in my prediction of what will interest me and happen to me. So here's the link to my 2020 post about my expected 2021(https://www.ravirer.com/2020/12/18/topics-to-explore-in-2021/).

So, yes I did do my semester in Animation culturelle and I am technically finishing this certificate by the end of the 2022 winter semester. Nonetheless, I now consider to include Visual arts in my cursus, which would have me do a little bit more uni classes than what I had planned. 

I also did try TELUQ'S system and I hated it. Very out of date content. Something to forget about truly. It was an unpleasant experience. At least, I had my money back.

I did run for city counselor in Hochelaga and got 651 votes (7%) and I am proud of it. For sure, the experience who was the most releving for me this year. One article was published on my campagin https://latelier.uqam.media/les-defis-de-la-candidature-independante/ One thing I must say is that I underestimate the amout of mental energy the administrative work would take. 

So those were my few 'certainties' I had at the end of 2020 in regard of 2021. Linked to that, I expected myself to learn more about municipalism and the history of my neighborhood. I was also planning to read more about critical analysis of education. Well, I must say I didn't do that actively at all, haha.

By re-reading my list of 'I wish to accomplish/learn about', I realized I somewhat focused on thing I never thought I'd focus about this much. I don't want to go through all of them, because honestly it would feel defaitist, so I'll instead just dumped the thing that made me proud this year.

- I was proud to have taken the lead as the person in charge of inclusivity of AEUPAC, especially since it was a newly created role and that I knew nobody in this student association to begin with. But here's the thing that I made that makes me feel satisfied about the time I was in that position : adoption d'une position contre l'instrumentalisation de la liberté d'expression; adoption d'une motion qui rend obligatoire la reconnaissance du territoire et début de réunions du CE et des assemblées; consultation auprès des étudiant-e-s sur les enjeux d'inclusivité; amendement pour que les personnes QTBIPOC+ soient privilégiés dans l'embauche des présidiums de l'AEUPAC; conférence en ligne par Michelle Wouters _Art et activisme des femmes, 2-Spirits et Indigequeer des Premières Nations_

- I was also very excited by the fact that I somewhat have scored a real dream job this summer. I was working outside and/or from home with very little supervision. I've learned so much about gardening. I have met so many wonderful people (including lots of queers) from my neighborhood. With a good salary. And so much free food. Ah! 

- I fell so much in love with glitch art. I surprisingly even got a contract to do an affiche promo for Philémon Cimon!! I'm still very excited about my craft and I'm looking forward how my art will evolved in this world. In any case, I am very much excited to combine glitch art with poetry more thoroughly. 

- I also managed to do approximately 500$ as a 'self-employed' without even trying that much so it's a great start, especially since next year I hope I could count a bit more on that.
