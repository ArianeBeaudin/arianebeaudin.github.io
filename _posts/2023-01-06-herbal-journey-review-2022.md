As we are entering 2023, I feel like taking my herbal studies seriously since my big project this year revolves around herbalism. So I thought about writing here what I've studied while I don't have a physical plant journal.

So plant-wise, I could say that the new besties I made were Melisse and Mugwort. The year before (2021) my new found star was Agastache-fenouil and it was fun to continue deepen my relationship with it. 

In terms of crafting, I was proud to have made my first oil infusion : I made a basil infused olive oil. I was especially interested in the capacity of basil to lift one's mood and I must say that it did spark joy to cook with that oil so I am looking forward making it again. 

I also made my first tincture : it was Origan citronnée in vodka. I made it namely thinking that it would boost the immune system, I've been stuck with the idea that oregano can do that but I don't have bullet proof science for that lol, but honestly, it was more for the smell of the plant that I made the tincture. The thing is this variety smells kinda chemical, like it reminds me of my father's garage. So right now, I'm having fun taking drop of that tincture to 'have a sensorial experience' more than anything. Like I never enjoyed tincture that much because of the alcohol taste (this one was made with Vodka that I especially don't like, I hope I'll try making a tincture with rhum eventually), but this is something else haha, it makes me laugh usually. I've bought glycerine too to try make a tincture with that but I've read that it's like potent than alcohol-based one so that is sad. I also made a cone incent with dried sage! It wasn't mind-blowing but it worked.
